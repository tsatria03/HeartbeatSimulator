new in 1.0
Increased the bpm upper and lower limits to 40 and 160.
The bpm upper limit is now fixed to 150. The parameter can no longer go abuv this vallue.
The bpm  lower limit is now fixed to 50. The parameter can no longer go abuv this vallue.
The heartbeat timer will now pause along with it's sound if the window is not in focus, and resume when you go back into the program. This is useful for when you don't want it going in the backround wile you are using other apps.
You can now press the enter key to pause/resume the heartbeat timer. This will mute the sound being played as well.
Pressing the letter T will tell you what heartbeat sound you are currently focused on.
You can now change the heartbeat sound by pressing the left or right arrow key.
You can now press the letter H to tell you the exact bpm of the heartbeat sound.
This app currently lets you open it to use the up and down arrow keys to increase/decrease the heartbeat sound in beats per minute, or bpm, as some people would call it. You are allowed to press the escape key to exit the program when necessary.
